## Orduithe Seasta / Standing Orders

1 - The mover of a proposal shall speak for not more than three minutes.

2 - The seconder of a proposal shall speak for no more than three minutes.

3 - No motion shall be discussed for more than 20 minutes.  The motion must be disposed of when the 20 minutes have elapsed.

![](/images/documents/agm-2026/image4.jpg){width=540 height=400}

4 - The mover shall have the right of reply in all cases.  2 minutes maximum.

5 - Voting shall be by a show of hands on all motions.  A simple majority shall ensure the adoption of any item.  Where rule changes are proposed, a two thirds majority shall be necessary in every instance to ensure their adoption.

6 - The usual method for the disposal of amendments and agenda shall be followed.

7 - In the event of the proposition "That the question be put", and that this proposition be seconded, the presiding Cathaoirleach shall on a show of hands either continue the debate or 'Put the question'.

8 - In the event of the proposition "That the AGM, move to next business " and that this proposition be seconded, the presiding Cathaoirleach shall put the proposition to a vote immediately.  If defeated the debate shall be resumed.

9 - If a position on the Committee be contested, voting shall be by secret ballot.  Each officer will be deemed elected on the ‘First Past the Post’ system.

10 - The chairman may at his discretion ask if there is no obvious objection to a motion that the motion be put in order, to save time.

11 -   The chairman may at his discretion and in consultation with order officers change the order of business in the interest of efficiency.

12.  The order of business may also be changed at the request of a majority of delegates present and voting.

13.   Only club members may speak at the AGM or General Meeting. Other speakers may only address the AGM when prior arrangements are made through the Committee

### Our Sporting Year

Our Senior Men competed this year in a highly competitive Division 1B, with the season producing a mixture of challenging results and some excellent performances. The lads demonstrated their ability to compete with the strongest sides in the division, recording some impressive victories and pushing teams closely throughout the campaign. While consistency sometimes proved difficult, the season provided valuable experience across the panel and positives to build upon. The determination shown throughout the year saw them retain their division 1B status for 2027 and leaves us optimistic that this group can continue to develop and push forward in the seasons ahead.

Our Senior Ladies enjoyed a positive and competitive campaign in Division 2 this year, producing some strong performances and demonstrating their ability to compete strongly throughout the season. With six victories from ten league games, the ladies showed consistency, determination and plenty of attacking quality, ultimately securing an impressive third-place finish. The season provided further opportunities for the squad to develop and build together, with plenty of positives to take forward.

Following a successful Junior campaign that we had expected promotion to Division 3A, an unexpected curveball saw the introduction of the new Seconds League. While perhaps not the outcome we had anticipated, the lads embraced the challenge. Mid-season league split brought the opportunity for competitive football, the team responded strongly, finishing an impressive 2nd place in Group 1 of the Seconds League. We await in anticipation for the proposed structure for Seconds and Junior football in 2027. We welcome competitive football and the opportunity for this group of lads to continue to compete and shine. Our Championship campaign also got off to a flying start before a waterlogged pitch brought proceedings to an unexpected halt just as the lads were finding their rhythm. The rearranged fixture unfortunately left us with a slightly reduced squad, but the lads battled right to the end and were just pipped at the post by Clann Éireann away from home.

Our underage teams enjoyed another positive year, with the U16 Girls securing the Division 2 League title, while our Minor Boys and Féile Boys both progressed to Championship semi-finals, with the Féile lads narrowly losing by a single point to the eventual winners. Strong league campaigns and continued development across our Minor, U16 and U14 boys’ and girls’ teams demonstrate the talent coming through our underage structures. These performances are a credit to the commitment of our young players and coaches and give us great confidence for the future of the club.

At county level, we have been delighted to welcome Tyrone Senior player Jayne (Lyons) Hearty, who has made Silverbridge her new club home. Jayne has settled in extremely well and has been a fantastic addition to our Senior Ladies squad. Throughout the year, we were immensely proud to support our senior county representatives Niamh Reel, Cailin Traynor, Jayne (Lyons) Hearty with Tyrone, Jarly Óg Burns and Sean Conlon.

There were some very special moments along the way, not least both the Armagh Ladies and Men’s Senior teams being crowned Ulster Champions, with the men ending an 18-year wait for the provincial title. A particular highlight for our club was Jarly Óg making history by becoming the first Silverbridge player to be named a PwC GAA/GPA Football All-Star. This remarkable achievement is a testament not only to his talent and dedication, but also to the humility and character he continues to show.

We are incredibly proud and fortunate to have players of this calibre representing our club and, more importantly, acting as such positive role models for our younger players. In addition, our presence in the Orchard jersey at underage level has been equally encouraging, demonstrating the strength of our player pathway and the bright future that lies ahead.

### A club, A Community, A Space for All

Our club continues to thrive as a vibrant community hub, bringing people of all ages together through a wide range of cultural, social, recreational and wellbeing activities. Our Healthy Clubs and Green Initiatives continue to go from strength to strength, with a wide range of programmes promoting physical and mental wellbeing, sustainability and community engagement. These have included mental health awareness programmes, online safety awareness, the launch of our walking club, a sports gear swap shop, potting plants, and community strength and balance classes, ensuring there is something for everyone.

These initiatives are driven by the tireless dedication of Theresa McShane and our Healthy Clubs and Green Committee, whose hard work and commitment were recognised with the achievement of Green Club status, with Healthy club Bronze Club Status in sight, with ambition for Gold Status in the coming year.

Our facilities continue to provide an important home for a wide variety of community groups and activities. From Amazing Abilities Youth Club, our Senior Citizens Committee and talented pantomime performers, darts, bowls, badminton, taekwondo, Irish dancing, dance classes, Irish classes, Kids camps, the club remains a place where people of all generations can come together grab a cuppa, stay active, build friendships and feel part of something special.

We are immensely proud of the continued success of our Scór na nÓg Ballad Group. In 2025, this exceptionally talented young group brought the County Championship title back to Silverbridge Harps for the first time in almost nine years. Their spine-tingling performance at *Leaps & Lines* towards the end of last year gave us a glimpse of just how special this group was becoming. We were absolutely delighted to see them successfully retain their County Championship title this year, a remarkable achievement and a tremendous source of pride for everyone associated with the club.

All of these activities and achievements reflect what makes Silverbridge Harps so special. We are more than a GAA club. We are a community, a place of belonging and a space for everyone.

## Team Reports

### Senior Men’s Football Report 2026

#### Armagh Senior Football League, Division 1B

The 2026 season presented a challenging but competitive campaign for our Senior Men, who competed in Division 1B of the Armagh Senior Football League under the management of Eamon McEvoy, assisted by John McMahon and Ryan Boyle.

The league campaign provided some memorable performances and some closely contested encounters. One of the highlights came in May when Silverbridge recorded an impressive 1-18 to 0-20 victory over Clan na Gael, handing the Lurgan side their first defeat of the campaign. Pauric Keating scored the crucial goal as the team showed great determination to secure an important home win.

Another encouraging performance came against Granemore, where Silverbridge secured a convincing 1-17 to 0-06 victory. The team also recorded a narrow win over High Moss Sarsfields in July, demonstrating their ability to compete in tight games.

There were, however, some difficult results throughout the campaign. A particularly disappointing encounter came against Clan na Gael in July. Silverbridge made a superb start, scoring two early goals through Michael Traynor and Pauric Keating to establish a ten-point advantage. Despite holding a commanding position for much of the game, a late comeback from the home side saw Silverbridge narrowly defeated, 3-12 to 2-12.

The team also faced difficult tests against Carrickcruppen and Dromintee, highlighting the competitive nature of Division 1B. These matches provided valuable lessons and underlined the importance of maintaining consistency throughout the full seventy minutes.

Overall, the league campaign offered opportunities for the squad to develop and gain valuable experience against strong opposition. While results were mixed, the team demonstrated that they could compete with the leading sides in the division.

#### Armagh Senior Football Championship

Attention turned to the Senior Football Championship in August, with Silverbridge drawn in Group B alongside Cullyhanna, Killeavy and Clan na Gael.

The opening fixture saw Silverbridge face local rivals Cullyhanna. Despite their efforts, the team suffered a 1-17 to 0-10 defeat. Silverbridge were unable to find the scores required to get their campaign off to a positive start.

The second group game brought another South Armagh derby, this time against Killeavy. Silverbridge again faced a difficult challenge and, despite their efforts, were defeated. With two losses from their opening fixtures, the team’s hopes of progressing to the knockout stages were over before the final round.

The final group game against Clan na Gael in Lurgan provided a competitive and closely fought encounter. Both sides battled throughout, with Silverbridge showing resilience and determination. Tiernan Murphy produced an excellent second-half performance, registering four points, including a two-pointer. The game finished level at 0-13 apiece, with both sides having opportunities to claim victory in the closing stages.

Although the draw was a positive response following the opening two defeats, it was not enough to keep Silverbridge in the competition, and the team exited the championship at the group stage.

While the championship results were disappointing, the experience gained will be important as the squad looks to build for the future. Competing at senior level in Armagh requires consistency, commitment and resilience, and the challenge now is to learn from the campaign and return stronger.

#### Acknowledgements

Sincere thanks are extended to our management team, Eamon McEvoy, John McMahon and Ryan Boyle, for their commitment, preparation and dedication throughout the season. The time and effort involved in preparing a senior team each week is considerable and is greatly appreciated by the club.

Thanks to the backroom team, medical personnel, selectors and all those who assisted with match day preparations and team administration.

Finally, we thank our loyal supporters for their continued encouragement, both at home and away, and our sponsors for their invaluable support of the senior team.

As we look ahead to the 2027 season, the focus will be on building consistency, developing the squad and working together to achieve improved results in both league and championship football.

We wish the players and management every success in the season ahead.

The campaign also included challenging fixtures against strong opposition. Silverbridge narrowly lost to Clann Éireann II on 24 July by 0-13 to 0-17 before going down to Madden Raparees II on 9 August by 1-11 to 2-15.

Throughout the league, the second team provided valuable opportunities for players to gain competitive experience, develop their game and represent Silverbridge. The performances and victories demonstrated the squad’s attacking potential and the commitment of the players who made themselves available throughout the season.

#### Junior Reserve Championship

Following the league campaign, the team turned its attention to the Junior Reserve Championship, entering Group C with the aim of progressing to the knockout stages.

Silverbridge made an excellent start on 30 August, defeating Naomh Peadar by 1-15 to 1-4. The team followed this with another important victory on 6 September, overcoming Clan na Gael by 0-16 to 0-13 in a competitive encounter.

The group campaign concluded on 11 September with an impressive 2-13 to 0-8 win over Armagh Harps. Three victories from three matches gave Silverbridge an unbeaten group-stage record and secured their place in the semi-final.

On 1 October, Silverbridge faced Clann Éireann in the championship semi-final at Middletown. Despite a determined performance, the team was defeated by 1-12 to 0-12. Although the result brought the championship campaign to an end, reaching the semi-final after winning all three group matches was a notable achievement for the squad.

#### Acknowledgements

The club would like to thank Joe Crawley, Gerard Agnew and Peter Reel for managing the second team during the 2026 season. Their time, commitment and work with the players were greatly appreciated.

Thanks to those who supported the team throughout the league and championship campaigns. The seconds team continued to play an important role within Silverbridge GFC, providing players with the opportunity to develop, compete and contribute to the club. The unbeaten championship group campaign and semi-final appearance were positive highlights of the season.

The club congratulates everyone involved and looks forward to building on the progress made during 2026.
